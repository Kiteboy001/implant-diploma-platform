# The Implant Diploma — Full Programme Reference

Source: https://theimplantdiploma.co.uk/ (scraped May 2026)

## Programme Facts

- **Course:** The Implant Diploma, Level 11 / EQF Level 7
- **Duration:** 2 years, 12 modules × 2-day blocks = 24 teaching days
- **Cohort:** 2025–2027 cohort full; 2026 waiting list open
- **Tutors:** 7 tutors
- **Hospital:** 3 days dental hospital exposure
- **Live patients:** Students place/restore implants under supervision
- **Guarantee:** Minimum 9 implant placements during the course
- **Placement start:** Module 4 (first live patient placement)
- **Diploma requirement:** 4 documented clinical cases
- **Advanced certification:** 2 additional advanced procedures

## Module Breakdown

1. Record keeping, consent, documentation, Megagen implant system
2. Asepsis, flap design, osteotomy, suturing
3. Treatment planning, CBCT interpretation
4. Single implant placement (students begin placing on patients)
5. Implant restorations, impression techniques
6. GBR and grafting principles
7. Sinus grafting overview
8. Full arch / All-on-X concepts
9. Block grafts and advanced grafting
10. Complications, peri-implantitis management
11. Occlusion, advanced restoration
12. Case presentations, OSCE assessments, viva exams

- **Viva voce** exams
